# Roles and Permissions System

## Assigned Roles
- `super_admin`: Full administrative access.
- `secretary_general`: Executive governance and official statements.
- `finance_manager`: Payment approval, financial reports, ledger management.
- `social_manager`: Takaful social support cases review.
- `genealogy_manager`: Lineage and family tree verification.
- `media_manager`: News and media archive.
- `member`: Standard registered member.
